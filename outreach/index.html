PhantomHunt

Three phantoms hunt one human in a tactical multiplayer chase through a haunted castle.

Trailer

Gameplay

Developers

  • Jan Haag
  • Hermes Reisner
  • Vera Bitterlin
  • Silas Weber
  • Ismail Djemaili

Controls

WASD / left stick: move

R: use Wisdom Blessing when available

Abilities

Glitch power-up: if the human touches a phantom while active, the phantom is teleported and the human gains points.

Wisdom Blessing: blinds all phantoms for a short time.
